Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Cookie & Storage Policy explains how AOLA Smart QR Ordering ("AOLA", "we", "us") utilizes cookies, HTML5 local storage, and related browser technologies to deliver a fast, frictionless dining experience without compromising diner privacy.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by websites you visit. Modern web applications also utilize HTML5 Local Storage and Session Storage, which securely save data directly within your browser. Because AOLA operates without requiring diners to download a mobile app or create an account, local browser storage is essential to remember your table and cart while you browse.

2. Categories of Storage We Use

We classify our browser storage usage into two primary categories:

A. Strictly Essential Storage (Always Required)

These items are vital for the core functionality of contactless QR ordering. Without them, you cannot add dishes to your cart or submit an order to the kitchen.

Storage Key / Cookie Type Purpose Duration
AOLA_CART_ITEMS Local Storage Stores your selected food and drink items before placing your order. Session / Cleared upon order placement
AOLA_TABLE_NUM Local Storage Remembers your dining table number so food is delivered correctly. Persistent until changed
AOLA_SHOP_ID Local Storage Identifies the restaurant whose menu you are currently viewing. Persistent until changed
AOLA_COOKIE_CONSENT Local Storage Records your response to our cookie and storage consent banner. 180 Days
AOLA_OWNER_SHOP / AOLA_ADMIN_PIN Session Storage Merchant and manager authentication session tokens. Deleted when browser tab closes

B. Operational Health & Crash Diagnostics

To detect server outages, failed payment network calls, and UI crashes in real-time, AOLA records anonymous operational telemetry:

  • AOLA_LOCAL_LOGS_V1: A circular in-memory/local buffer limited to the last 100 events (such as successful order dispatch or network retry). All entries are deeply sanitized to strip passwords, PINs, tokens, or financial numbers before storage. Logs are automatically aged out after 30 days.

3. Third-Party Cookies (Razorpay Gateway)

When you choose to pay your dining bill online via UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, or NetBanking, the payment is securely handled by Razorpay via an encrypted checkout frame. Razorpay may deploy fraud-prevention and security cookies required for PCI-DSS financial transaction compliance.

4. What We NEVER Do

  • We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels (e.g., Facebook Pixel, Google Ads remarketing).
  • We do not track your browsing activity across other websites.
  • We do not sell, broker, or rent diner behavioral data to marketing companies.

5. Managing and Clearing Storage

You can manage, restrict, or clear your cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings:

  • Safari (iOS / macOS): Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → Remove All.
  • Chrome / Android: Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Browsing Data → Cookies and site data.
  • Private / Incognito Browsing: Opening the dining QR link in private browsing mode automatically discards all session cookies and storage when you close the tab.
