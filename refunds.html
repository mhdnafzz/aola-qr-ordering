Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Thank you for dining with AOLA Smart QR Ordering. Because our platform facilitates real-time restaurant orders transmitted immediately to the kitchen staff, please review our refund and cancellation terms below.

1. Order Cancellation

Due to the perishable nature of freshly prepared food and beverage items:

  • Orders may be cancelled only if the kitchen staff has not yet accepted or begun food preparation.
  • Once an order status shifts to "Preparing" or "Cooking" on the live kitchen display, cancellations cannot be processed.
  • To request an immediate cancellation, customers must notify restaurant floor staff or the head chef immediately at their table.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds will be granted in the following scenarios:

  • Duplicate Transactions: If your bank account or card was debited multiple times for a single order due to a network glitch.
  • Item Out of Stock: If an ordered item cannot be served by the kitchen after successful payment.
  • Quality Issues or Incorrect Item: Subject to verification and approval by the on-site restaurant manager.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment source (Credit/Debit Card, UPI, or NetBanking) via Razorpay. Typically, the credit reflects in your bank account within 5 to 7 business days depending on your issuing bank.
