Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At AOLA Smart QR Ordering ("AOLA"), we value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, utilize, and protect user data when diners and restaurant merchants interact with our contactless ordering platform.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data footprint for diners. When placing orders, we may collect:

  • Table Number and selected menu items with cooking notes.
  • Customer contact info (such as phone number or name) only when explicitly provided for billing, receipt delivery, or loyalty updates.
  • Technical diagnostic data (browser type, session timestamp, anonymous IP) necessary for order routing and bot prevention.

2. Payment Information Security

We never store your credit card numbers, debit card PINs, CVVs, or NetBanking passwords on our servers. All financial transactions are encrypted and transmitted directly through Razorpay, which is compliant with PCI-DSS standards.

3. How We Use Your Data

Collected information is strictly used to:

  • Dispatch orders directly to the restaurant kitchen display system.
  • Generate and deliver digital receipts.
  • Prevent malicious automated scripts and security fraud.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared exclusively with the restaurant merchant where the order is placed and the licensed payment gateway (Razorpay) for financial settlement.
